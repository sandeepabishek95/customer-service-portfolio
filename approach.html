My resolution process

From first contact to a clear outcome

A structured process helps me remain calm, organised and customer-focused, even when a request is urgent.

STEP 01

Understand

Listen to the concern, clarify the symptoms and establish the impact on the user or business.

STEP 02

Investigate

Gather evidence, check relevant systems and follow an appropriate troubleshooting process.

STEP 03

Act & Communicate

Apply an approved solution where possible, or escalate with clear notes and regular updates.

STEP 04

Confirm & Document

Confirm the outcome with the user, update the ticket and record useful lessons for future reference.

When situations get difficult

Calm, structured and professional

WHEN A CUSTOMER IS FRUSTRATED

Show empathy and listen

I allow the customer to explain the concern, acknowledge the impact and avoid making promises before understanding what can realistically be done.

WHEN A PROBLEM IS UNRESOLVED

Escalate with context

I document the symptoms, checks performed, impact and relevant evidence so the next team can continue the investigation without unnecessary repetition.

WHEN PRIORITIES COMPETE

Focus on impact

I consider urgency, business impact and service commitments, communicate expectations and seek guidance when priorities conflict.

WHEN AN ISSUE REPEATS

Look beyond the symptom

I review previous incidents and documented findings, then support root cause investigation and preventive actions where appropriate.
